# videofab

**videofab** is a private, internal tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel **"Por qué será"**, a Spanish-language educational channel about everyday science and curiosities.

## What it does

videofab runs locally on the channel owner's personal computer. It helps produce educational videos and, after the owner has manually reviewed and approved each one, uploads it to the owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API, setting:

- the video file, title, description and tags
- a custom thumbnail
- the privacy status and scheduled publish time

## Who can use it

videofab is **not a public service**. It has no user accounts, no sign-up and no public interface. It is used exclusively by the channel owner, who authorizes it with their own Google account through OAuth 2.0. It does not access any other user's or channel's data.

> This tool uses the **YouTube API Services** to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.
